Privacy Policy

# Privacy Policy **Last updated:** January 2026 AIAvatar ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting it. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your information when you use the **AIAvatar** mobile application. --- ## 1. Information We Collect ### 1.1 Device-Based Identifier AIAvatar uses a device-based identifier to associate generated avatars and credits with your device. No account registration, email address, or login is required. ### 1.2 User-Provided Content When you use AIAvatar, you may upload photos for avatar generation. * Uploaded photos are used **only** for avatar generation. * Original uploaded photos are **deleted after generation completes**. * Generated avatars are stored so you can access them later. ### 1.3 Automatically Collected Data We may collect limited technical data to improve app stability and performance, including: * App usage data * Crash reports * Performance diagnostics This data does not directly identify you. --- ## 2. How We Use Your Information We use collected data to: * Generate AI-powered avatars * Manage credits and avatar unlocking * Deliver notifications when avatar generation is complete * Improve app reliability and performance * Comply with legal and App Store requirements --- ## 3. AI & Image Processing AIAvatar uses **Google Gemini APIs** to generate avatars. * Uploaded images are securely sent to Google Gemini for processing. * Your images are **not used to train AI models**. * Processing is performed only to generate your requested avatars. You can review Google’s privacy practices here: * **Google Gemini API Privacy Policy:** [https://policies.google.com/privacy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) --- ## 4. Payments & Purchases AIAvatar offers optional in-app purchases for credits. * All payments are processed securely by **Apple In-App Purchases**. * We do **not** collect or store payment information. * Apple handles all billing, refunds, and transaction security. --- ## 5. Analytics & Crash Reporting We use Firebase services to improve app quality: * **Firebase Analytics** – to understand app usage trends * **Firebase Crashlytics** – to diagnose crashes and technical issues These services collect anonymized usage and diagnostic data in accordance with Firebase’s privacy policy. --- ## 6. Push Notifications AIAvatar may send push notifications to: * Notify you when avatar generation is complete * Provide important app-related updates Notifications are optional and can be disabled in your device settings. --- ## 7. Data Storage & Security We implement reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your data against unauthorized access, loss, or misuse. Data is stored using secure cloud infrastructure. --- ## 8. Data Deletion You are in control of your data. * You can delete generation tasks and associated avatars directly within the app. * Deleting a task permanently removes related stored data. Once deleted, the data cannot be recovered. --- ## 9. Children’s Privacy AIAvatar is suitable for users aged **4 and above**. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe that a child has provided personal data through the app, please contact us and we will take appropriate action. --- ## 10. Changes to This Policy We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date. Continued use of the app after changes means you accept the updated policy. --- ## 11.
